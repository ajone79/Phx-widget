**Full description (4000 max):**
A clean home-screen widget that shows what is happening in your game right now and what starts next, for your ops level.

WHAT IT SHOWS
- NOW: events running right now, with a countdown to when each one ends
- NEXT: what starts next, with a countdown
- Your alliance's next 2 territory capture times
- The next incursion and the next alliance tournament
- Optional: the next 2 raid territory times (turn on in settings)
- Maintenance warnings

BUILT FOR YOUR LEVEL
Pick your ops level once and the widget only shows events you can take part in. Similar events (such as the mission and leaderboard versions) are merged into one line.

THE 4PM / 5PM PROBLEM, SORTED
Some events switch over at 16:00 and some at 17:00 because of the clock change. The widget treats them as the same daily reset, so Now and Next line up with your real game day.

EXTRAS
- Dark, space-themed design with background artwork
- Countdown refresh of 1, 5 or 15 minutes
- Filters for passes, banners and long-running events
- Incursion region (EU, US, APAC)
- Time travel demo mode to preview the widget as it was days ago
- Works offline from the last download

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
