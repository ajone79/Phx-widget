# Phx Widget privacy policy

Phx Widget shows game event times (events, territory captures, incursions, tournaments) on your home screen. It is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's publisher.

**What stays on your phone:** your settings (ops level and display options) and a cached copy of the event data.

**What is sent:** the app downloads public event data from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) and public alliance schedule data from a Supabase service. These requests contain no personal information, but, like any web request, the servers can see your IP address.

**What we do not collect:** no name, email, account, location, device identifiers, analytics or advertising data. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not sell or share personal data.

**Logs:** the hosting services above may keep standard request logs (such as IP address) for a short period for operation and abuse prevention.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children under 13.
